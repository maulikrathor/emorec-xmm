# KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md

**Why this document exists:** the honest, versioned list of what this system cannot do and why — pre-registered where possible, updated with evidence as experiments land. It feeds the model card, the report's limitations section, and interview answers (stating limitations crisply is a maturity signal). Never delete an entry; mark it "confirmed (evidence: …)", "not observed", or state that it was not evaluated.

**Status as of CC-10 (final).** One full-train seed-42 run per model (B1, B2, M) exists; evidence paths are under `results/`. Narrative and numbers: `docs/FINAL_REPORT.md`. "Not evaluated" means the experiment that would test the entry was not run, so the entry stands as a pre-registered caveat.

## Dataset-inherited
1. **Text dominance of MELD.** Pre-registered expectation: text carries most signal; multimodal gains small and class-concentrated. **Status: confirmed (evidence: `results/reports/statistics.md`, `results/reports/ablation.md`).** B1 text-only reaches test weighted-F1 0.6015 vs B2 audio-only 0.4273; zeroing the text costs M 0.3519 weighted-F1 while zeroing the audio costs 0.0373; M's test gain over B1 is +0.0158 weighted-F1 (McNemar p = 0.021) and concentrated in fear/disgust (`results/runs/*/classification_report_test.txt`).
2. **Noisy audio channel.** Laugh track, overlapping speakers, background music; no source separation by design (RA8). Failure mode to be characterized in E9. **Status: not evaluated (E9 not run).** Indirect evidence only: audio-only is weak (test weighted-F1 0.4273; F1 0.00 on fear and disgust on both splits; test accuracy 0.4797 is below the all-neutral accuracy 0.4812) (`results/reports/statistics.md`, `results/reports/meld_metadata.md` R1).
3. **Severe class imbalance.** Fear/disgust have few hundred train samples; per-class F1 for these will be weak regardless of weighting. **Status: confirmed (evidence: `results/runs/*/classification_report*.txt`).** B1 disgust F1 is 0.00 on dev and test and fear 0.04 / 0.10; M reaches fear 0.25 / 0.21 and disgust 0.21 / 0.14 (dev / test), still low, on very small supports (dev fear 40, disgust 22).
4. **Label noise.** MELD emotion labels are annotator judgments of acted performances; inter-annotator disagreement is documented in the original paper. Taken as ground truth by policy (A7); discussed, not fixed. **Status: not evaluated** (no annotator-agreement data in the repo).
5. **Domain narrowness.** Scripted US sitcom English, small recurring cast, 1990s-2000s conversational register. No claim of generalization beyond it (see MODEL_CARD restrictions). **Status: not evaluated** (no out-of-domain test); unchanged.
6. **Speaker priors.** Main-cast emotion distributions differ; speaker features are deliberately excluded (RA9) but names appear in text context — residual leakage is possible. **Status: partially evidenced; leakage not quantified.** 156 (Season, Episode) pairs occur in more than one split and 5 (Season, Episode, StartTime, EndTime) keys occur in two splits (`results/reports/meld_metadata.md` R4, R5), so cast and episodes recur across splits; no experiment measured how much of the performance this explains.

## Model/architecture
7. **Frozen encoders (Stage 1)** cap achievable F1 below full-fine-tune SOTA; accepted trade (ED1). LoRA may close part of the gap (E6, conditional). **Status: not evaluated (E6/LoRA not run).** M's best test weighted-F1 is 0.6173 (`results/reports/statistics.md`); how much of any gap is due to freezing is unmeasured.
8. **k=0 at demo time**: live inputs get no dialogue context; demo accuracy < benchmark accuracy by construction. Stated in the UI. **Status: not evaluated** (`scripts/demo_predict.py` exists but no k=0 accuracy was measured, and no Gradio demo was built).
9. **10 s audio cap** truncates ~3% of utterances (start-truncated by ED5); long-utterance behavior slightly degraded. **Status: not evaluated** (the truncation rate and its effect are not measured in `results/`).
10. **7-class closed set**: no neutral-vs-ambiguous abstention, no mixed emotions, no intensity. Calibration unstudied (FUTURE_WORK E-ext6). **Status: unchanged; calibration still not studied.**

## Explainability
11. **Post-hoc attributions describe model behavior, not causal ground truth about emotion.** Faithfulness is measured, not assumed — and faithfulness ≠ human-plausibility. **Status: confirmed in kind (evidence: `results/reports/faithfulness.md`, `results/reports/ablation.md`).** IG beats a random control on all four deletion curves, but its mean |convergence delta| is 0.284 (text-driven), and no human-plausibility study was run.
12. **Attention-as-explanation is known-unreliable**; it is included precisely to measure its disagreement with IG/occlusion, not as a trusted method. **Status: not evaluated** (attention attribution was not implemented; `Output.xattn` is never populated).
13. **IG-audio interpretability risk** (R7): frame-level attribution may be diffuse even post-projection; occlusion arbitrates; a negative faithfulness result for IG-audio is reported as a finding. **Status: not observed in the faithfulness evaluation (evidence: `results/reports/faithfulness.md`).** IG-audio beats the random control on comprehensiveness (+0.0534, paired CI [+0.0409, +0.0640]) and sufficiency (−0.0472 [−0.0584, −0.0364]) and passes the head-randomisation check (mean signed Spearman −0.0798). Caveats: attribution was taken at the pre-`proj_a` frame output, not post-`proj_a`; occlusion, the intended arbiter, was not run; per-0.5 s-bin audio scores in `results/reports/xai_examples.md` are small next to token scores and were not interpreted further.
14. **Deletion-metric circularity caveat**: deletion operators match IG baselines by construction (documented choice in EVALUATION §5), which mildly favors IG in faithfulness comparisons; stated openly in the report. **Status: confirmed as a design property (evidence: `results/reports/faithfulness.md` header).** No second deletion operator was run, so its size is unmeasured; only IG was evaluated, so no cross-method comparison was biased.

## Serving/benchmark
15. **Train/serve ASR skew** (ED10): trained on gold transcripts, served through Whisper; degradation measured only if E8 runs. **Status: not evaluated** (no ASR path or E8 was built).
16. **Latency numbers are single-machine, batch-1** — indicative, not a throughput study. **Status: not evaluated** (no latency benchmark was run; wall times of training are in `results/runs/*/run_meta.json` only).
17. **Gemini benchmark is a point-in-time snapshot** (model version, pricing, prompt v1); results dated and price-sheet-snapshotted, not claimed as durable truths. **Status: not evaluated** (the Gemini benchmark was not run).

## Added by the final results (CC-10)
18. **Single seed.** All results are one run per model (seed 42); no seed-to-seed variance. Bootstrap CIs and McNemar quantify evaluation-set sampling, not training randomness. The 3-seed protocol of EVALUATION §3 was not run. (evidence: `results/runs/*/run_meta.json`, seed 42 only)
19. **Dev advantage is an upper estimate.** M beats B1 by +0.0607 weighted-F1 on dev but +0.0158 on test. M's dev score is the best of eight evaluated epochs; however, M's test score (0.6173) is not below its dev score (0.6155) while B1 scores higher on test (0.6015) than on dev (0.5548), so selection optimism and split difficulty cannot be separated with one seed. (evidence: `results/reports/statistics.md`, `results/runs/xattn-seed42-*/metrics.json`)
20. **Overlapping test CIs with a significant paired test.** M and B1 test weighted-F1 CIs overlap ([0.5973, 0.6367] vs [0.5815, 0.6193]) while McNemar is significant (p = 0.021). McNemar tests correctness (accuracy), not weighted-F1; a paired bootstrap of the weighted-F1 difference was not computed. The effect is small. (evidence: `results/reports/statistics.md`)
21. **Gate is not a valid importance signal for this architecture.** g_audio mean 0.0013 (99.91% below 0.05, 0% audio-dominant) while zeroing audio lowers weighted-F1 by 0.0373 (p = 5.43e-07) and shuffling it by 0.0296 (p = 0.015). The gate is applied after the cross-modal blocks, so it weights the pooled audio summary, not audio information. The mechanism is the architectural explanation; it was not isolated by a dedicated experiment, and an MLP-amplified small gated summary is not excluded. (evidence: `results/reports/gate_analysis.md`, `results/reports/ablation.md`)
22. **Ablation inputs are out-of-distribution.** The model never saw zeroed audio or all-pad text; ablation drops measure reliance under the intervention, not information content. Zeroing and shuffling were not tested against each other (their CIs overlap). (evidence: `results/reports/ablation.md`)
23. **IG convergence.** Mean |delta| = 0.284 over 80 passes at 64 steps; in the 20 example reports the text deltas reach 1.2 in magnitude while audio deltas are ≤ ~3e-06. The re-run rule never fired (it only applies below 64 steps). Individual token scores should not be over-interpreted. Faithfulness used 40 examples. (evidence: `results/reports/faithfulness.md`, `results/reports/xai_examples.md`)
24. **Sadness regression.** M is worse than B1 on sadness on both splits (dev F1 0.38 → 0.31; test 0.31 → 0.22, four-decimal 0.3128 → 0.2166), a recall loss (recall 0.34 → 0.22 dev, 0.27 → 0.14 test); cause not investigated. (evidence: `results/runs/*/classification_report*.txt`)
25. **Disgust remains weak, per-class numbers are noisy.** M disgust F1 0.21 dev / 0.14 test on supports of 22 / 68; no per-class confidence intervals were computed. Surprise and anger gains are clear on dev (+0.09 each) but marginal on test (+0.02, +0.01). (evidence: `results/runs/*/classification_report*.txt`)
26. **Baselines stopped at the 10-epoch cap.** B1 and B2 ran all 10 epochs with dev weighted-F1 still creeping up (B1 0.5529 → 0.5525 → 0.5548 at epochs 8–10); M stopped early at epoch 8 (best epoch 5). B3 (concat) was not run, so the M-vs-B3 comparison of EVALUATION §2 is missing. (evidence: `results/runs/*/metrics.json`)
27. **Data losses.** One dev utterance has no media (usable dev 1,108) and one train utterance failed audio extraction (usable train 9,988). (evidence: `results/reports/excluded.json`, `results/reports/media_inventory.md`)
28. **Provenance of `results/`.** `results/reports/final_results.md`'s per-class table is headed "dev" but contains the test per-class F1 values (its columns match `classification_report_test.txt`); use the per-run classification reports for per-split numbers. The script that produced `results/reports/statistics.md` is not committed (its numbers reproduce exactly with `emorec.training.metrics`, see `docs/FINAL_REPORT.md` §9). The three runs record git SHA `efb2451`, the code at training time. (evidence: the files named)
29. **Documentation inaccuracy: trainable parameters.** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the handoff say "≈2.3 M"; the implemented model has 3,688,969 trainable parameters (counted by instantiating the architecture; not stored in `results/`). Does not affect results. (evidence: `docs/FINAL_REPORT.md` §3)
